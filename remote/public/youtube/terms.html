Use and terms

Updated 16 November 2026

Sitting Room TV is a private household app maintained by Rupert Laycock. It is independently developed and is not made or endorsed by Google, YouTube or Apple.

The app provides TV browsing and controls. Connecting a YouTube account is optional and requires your permission in Google's own authorisation page. You can remove the connection from the phone app or your Google account.

The YouTube integration uses YouTube API Services. Users are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's handling of the YouTube connection is described in our privacy notice.

Opening a video does not change the account selected in the TV's YouTube app or transfer a subscription between accounts. This app does not supply paid subscriptions or guarantee the availability of videos or other content.
