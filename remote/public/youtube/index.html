Your TV, from your phone.

Sitting Room TV is a privately installed household TV browser and iPhone remote, maintained by Rupert Laycock. It brings film and TV browsing, YouTube search and remote controls together, with a choice of Apple TV or Android TV.

A separate YouTube connection on each phone

Connect your Google account to browse your subscriptions, channel videos, your own playlists and liked videos. You choose the account in Google's own sign-in page. Access is read-only.

Each paired phone keeps its own connection. You can disconnect and connect a different account without reinstalling the app. Videos open in YouTube on the selected TV platform, using the account already selected in that TV's YouTube app.

Google does not provide this app with Watch History or Watch Later. Public YouTube search works without an account connection.

Your real YouTube Home

The native iPhone app can display YouTube's own Home website in the Watch panel, where you sign in and browse your personalised recommendations. Selecting a video opens it on the selected TV platform. The separate Safari browser shortcut remains available. Website sign-ins stay on the phone and are separate from the optional library connection above.

This page provides information about the private app. It does not provide a public remote, account sign-in or app download.
